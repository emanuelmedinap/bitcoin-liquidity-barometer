System liquidity vs. bank Net Interest Income

Does system liquidity — the money supply, policy rates, and the yield-curve slope — drive US bank net interest income (NII), with bitcoin a barometer of that liquidity rather than a cause of bank revenue?

Method. A quarter-aligned panel of US public banks (SEC EDGAR XBRL) joined to FRED liquidity series (M2, fed funds, yield slope) and bitcoin; modeled in growth/changes rather than levels, with a temporal split and no imputation.
